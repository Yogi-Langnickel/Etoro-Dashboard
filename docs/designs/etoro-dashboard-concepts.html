Next implementation gate

Acceptance criteria

These checks define when a selected design is ready to implement. They intentionally favor trust, clarity, and restraint over finance-terminal density.

Persistent safety

Global header shows read-only, demo/real, provider state, last refresh, and stale/cache state.

No trading language

No buy, sell, close, copy, rebalance, recommend, optimize, execute, or best-move phrasing.

Receipts everywhere

Every displayed fact carries source type, freshness, retrieved time, or unavailable state.

Plain copy

Use fresh, stale, source missing, fixture, read-only, and needs review before expert terms.

Designed empty states

Fixture, stale, rate-limited, provider-offline, missing, and partial data states are first-class.

Privacy boundary

No account IDs, position IDs, exact balances, raw payloads, screenshots, credentials, or private exports.
