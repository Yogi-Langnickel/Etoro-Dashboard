Six different dashboard directions

These mocks are intentionally different operating models, not theme variations. All content is synthetic, privacy-safe, and framed as inspection context rather than trading guidance.

Control Tower Personal Ledger Risk Weather Research Dossier Simulation Workshop Spreadsheet Inspector

Shared safety frame

Each mock keeps credentials server-side, hides private identifiers, avoids exact private balances, and treats research as source context.

Always visible Mode, read-only lock, freshness, provider state, cache/backoff state.
Never shown Keys, account IDs, position IDs, raw payloads, real screenshots.
No action language No buy, sell, close, copy, rebalance, optimize, best move, or recommendation.
Every fact has receipt Source, freshness, coverage state, retrieved time, or unavailable state.
